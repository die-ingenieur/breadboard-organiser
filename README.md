**breadboard+components for referencing purposes. 
## What are the main components:
- A simple drawer system
- scissor mechanism
- a place to place your breadboard and a breadboard power supply (bc they are cheap enough for the most basic projects anyways)
- a place to specifically put an arduino uno
## how can you make it???
- simply just 3d print the files
- the drawers and casing can be printed and then simply just place the drawers inside the case , no other steps
- the bread board holder also requires no further steps
- for the power supply it is a bit more complex, you will (according to my design) need some breadboard female to male jumper cables.
  Just plug them into the power supply and use them as power supply sticks
